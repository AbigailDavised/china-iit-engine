# China Individual Income Tax (IIT) & Expat Calculator

Interactive client-side calculator powered by [china-iit-engine](https://www.npmjs.com/package/china-iit-engine) and maintained by [ChinaTaxCalc.com](https://chinataxcalc.com/calculator).

- **7-Bracket Comprehensive IIT**: 3% to 45% progressive schedule with 60,000 RMB annual standard deduction.
- **Annual Bonus Preferential Method**: Separate taxation calculation valid through Dec 31, 2027.
- **6-City Social Security Caps**: Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Hangzhou, Chengdu (with expat opt-out mode).
- **Expat BIA Allowances & GBA 15% Subsidy**: Models non-taxable housing/tuition/meal allowances and Greater Bay Area 15% ceiling refunds.
